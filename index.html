Questions

Good questions, plain answers

Is $5.99 a subscription?

No. It is a one-time payment of $5.99 through Square. There is no monthly bill and nothing renews.

Do I need to download anything from an app store?

No. After checkout you get a link. Open it on your phone and add it to your home screen using the steps above — it then opens just like a regular app.

How does the photo part work?

Take a photo of your cup, glass, bottle, can, or bowl — or choose a photo you already took. Snap Nutra estimates how much fluid is in it and adds it to your day. Always glance at the estimate: if it looks off, use Fix in Today's list or add the drink by hand instead.

What if my dry weight changes?

Just change it in the app. Dry weight and weight limit are both saved on the main screen and editable any time. Your dry weight never changes on its own — drinking only moves the separate “dry weight + fluid today” number and your line.

Where does my weight limit come from?

From you or your care team. Snap Nutra never invents or calculates a limit for you — you type in the weight limit in kilograms, and the app works out how much fluid that leaves you (limit − dry weight) and tracks you toward it.

Does soup or ice cream really count?

Yes — foods that melt or are mostly liquid count toward your fluid for the day. That is why broth, soup, ice cream / frozen treats, and ice cubes are all in the Add by hand list.

Is Snap Nutra medical advice?

No. Snap Nutra is a tracking helper. Its combined weight is a planning estimate — fluid also leaves your body — and photo amounts are estimates. Always follow the dry weight, limit, and guidance from your own care team, and talk to them about any health concerns.

A quick, honest note

Snap Nutra helps you track — it does not treat. The “dry weight + fluid today” number is a planning estimate to help you see where you stand against the limit you entered; it is not your actual body weight, because fluid also leaves your body through urine, sweat, and dialysis. Photo estimates can be wrong — check them, and use Fix or Add by hand when they are. If you ever feel unwell, contact your care team or emergency services, not an app.
